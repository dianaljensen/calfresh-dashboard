Application Trends

Rows expand from the section labels; Outcomes starts open. All applications, college student applications, and SSI household applications use different recipes — they cannot overlay on one chart. Student outcomes are share of applications disposed (approved or denied). Pended applications are still in process and sit on hover, not in the mix. When the nine denial-reason columns are close to Denied, the mix is Approved / Ineligible / Procedurally Denied / Other; otherwise Approved / Denied. SSI outcomes are Approved / Denied until ineligible and procedural are close to Denied, then that split (plus Other for a small leftover). Hover on a stacked mix includes the count those percents are of. SSI Denial details is households with only SSI recipients (not the some-SSI mix above). Channels uses GetCalFresh, BenefitsCal / Other Online, and Other. On SSI, GetCalFresh is labeled GetCalFresh (SSA) and is SSA_GCF_apps_submit subtracted from Online SSI. Age, Language, Race, Gender, Household size, and Exemptions & deductions are who applied that month, not approval rates by group. Exemptions and deductions overlap — they are shares claiming each type, not a 100% mix. CDSS replaces counts under 11 with an asterisk; those slices are plotted as 5. Hover percents then start with ~ and note that some data was masked. Larger hidden totals, and counties that did not report, stay blank. Student Denial details ungroups the large reason types (ineligible, over income, failed to provide income, failed determination, missed interview, procedural); Other is unavailable, out of the home, ineligible CF student, and residual.
Compare Places sits selected counties, regions, or size groups side by side for one population. Populations sits All / college student / SSI household applications side by side for one place. Mixes cannot overlay.

Data notes
  • CDSS hides small numbers to protect privacy. When that happens, we still show the mix, but those percents are estimates (they start with ~). If too much of a month is hidden, we leave it blank rather than guess.
  • Total applications is how many came in that month, from the CDSS Data Dashboard Monthly tab, not from CF296. The stacked percents are how that month’s decisions broke down; not the same count.
  • Channels (expandable row) use the same three names, but are calculated based on available variables for each population. See wonky & math-y details.
  • Age, Language, Race, Gender, Household size, and Exemptions & deductions (expandable rows) are who applied that month, not approval rates by group. College students have age, language, race, gender, and exemptions; SSI has language, race, household size, and deductions. Toggle # / % on the row; % is the default so columns stay comparable. Exemptions and deductions overlap (not a 100% mix). See wonky & math-y details.
  • Some CalWIN counties reported 0 SSI applications received from December 2019 through May 2022. SSI outcomes still exist those months.
  • SSI household outcomes are displayed according to best available data from the CDSS Data Dashboard Monthly tab. When ineligible + procedural is close to denied, the mix is shown as approved / ineligible / procedural / other. CalWIN counties ~2020–May 2022 show total denials, but not a break-out of ineligible vs. procedural, and are shown as a single lumped Denied in warm grey. Months where the ineligible / procedural split overshoots total denials are also shown this way.
  • SSI Denial details is households with only SSI recipients: ineligible vs procedural, a subset of some-SSI denials.
Wonky & math-y details

CF296 files

  • Current-era CF296 (FY2025-26+) and CF296_Legacy (FY2016-17 through FY2024-25) are combined by item meaning, not by cell number.

Small cells (*)

  • CDSS uses “*” for counts under 11, and sometimes for a larger complementary total.
  • If exactly one of the five outcome cells is starred, the missing value is recovered from disposed = approved + ineligible + procedural + withdrawn, when that number is over 10.
  • Remaining under-11 cells are plotted as 5, never shown as a count. Hover percents then start with ~.
  • Two or more large hidden cells, or a county listed as not reporting, stay blank. A complete month next to those gaps still draws.
  • Applications received and other volume cells are not plotted as 5.

Applications received

  • All applications: Applications Received on the CDSS Data Dashboard Monthly tab.
  • College students: New Applications Age Total. Not CF296 applications received, and not a checksum of approved + denied + pended.
  • SSI households: Online Apps - SSI + Non-Online Apps - SSI (at least some SSI, not the SSI-only pair). Reporting starts June 2019. Starred volume cells stay blank. CF296 has no SSI-specific application cells.

Channels

  • A 100% mix of GetCalFresh, BenefitsCal / Other Online, and Other, from Monthly-tab columns.
  • All applications: GetCalFresh = All_GCF_apps_submit; BenefitsCal / Other Online = Online Applications Received minus GetCalFresh; Other = Applications Received minus Online. After June 2025 GetCalFresh is 0; a dashed marker sits at July 2025 (the assister ended June 30, 2025).
  • College students: GetCalFresh = Applications Submitted via Code for America; BenefitsCal / Other Online = Other Online Source + BenefitsCal; Other = Other Source. Not CfA_GCF_apps_submit. Code for America continues after June 2025 at a small volume. The four source columns still sum to student approved + denied + pended.
  • SSI: GetCalFresh (SSA) = SSA_GCF_apps_submit (SSA office filings via GetCalFresh on behalf of SSI recipients). BenefitsCal / Other Online = Online Apps - SSI minus GetCalFresh (SSA). Other = Non-Online Apps - SSI. Last nonzero statewide SSA GCF is September 2024. If SSA GCF exceeds Online SSI, the month is left blank (CalWIN counties often reported 0 SSI online/non-online December 2019–May 2022 while SSA GCF was still filled).

College student outcomes

  • Share of applications disposed (approved + denied), starting January 2023. Pended is still in process: hover only, not in the mix.
  • When the nine Denial Reason columns are close to total denials, the mix is Approved / Ineligible / Procedurally Denied / Other.
    • Ineligible: ineligible + ineligible CF student + over income
    • Procedurally Denied: procedural + missed interview + failed to provide income + failed determination
    • Other: unavailable + out of the home + residual
  • Otherwise Approved / Denied of that two-part total (early 2023, when the reasons under-count).
  • Denial details ungroups the six large types. Other there is unavailable, out of the home, ineligible CF student, and residual. Named Ineligible + Procedural columns alone are only a subset of denials.

SSI household outcomes

  • Households with at least some SSI. No pended or withdrawn column.
  • When ineligible + procedural is close to denied: Approved / Ineligible / Procedurally Denied / Other.
  • Otherwise Approved / lumped Denied (warm grey): CalWIN ~2020–May 2022, and months where the split overshoots (including a bad November 2025 statewide ineligible reading).
  • Months that have approved and denied are still shown.
  • SSI Denial details is SSI Only - Ineligible Denials vs SSI Only Procedural Denials: only-SSI households, a subset of some-SSI denials.

Who applied (Age, Language, Race, Gender, Household size, Exemptions & deductions)

  • These mixes are composition of the applicant pool, not outcomes crossed by demographic. CDSS does not publish approved/denied by age, language, race, or gender.
  • College students, from the Monthly tab’s New Applications … block: Age (17 and under / 18–49 / 50 and over — CDSS names the middle band New Applications Age 8 to 49), Language (English / Spanish / Other), Race/ethnicity (nine CDSS categories), Gender (Female / Male / Other / Declined to state). Denominators are the matching Age / Language / Race / Gender Totals. Age Total is persons, not a checksum of approved + denied + pended. Hover on Age includes New Applications Avg Age.
  • SSI: Language is SSI Disposed HH Language - [X] (households). Race is SSI Person Race/Ethnicity - [X], persons in new apps disposed (the same total as SSI Persons in New Apps Disposed). No SSI age bands. Average age (Average Age of SSI Persons Newly Applying) sits on SSI household-size and volume hover. No SSI-app gender columns.
  • Household size is SSI-only households added that month: 1 / 2 / 3+ of SSI-only HHs of 1 added in the month, SSI-only HHs of 2 added in the month, and SSI only-HHs of 3+ added in the month (hyphen placement is CDSS’s). Denominator is Total SSI-only HHs added in the month. Not the some-SSI disposed mix on Outcomes.
  • Language Other lumps every listed language except English and Spanish (including CDSS Other and, for students, Missing).
  • Exemptions & deductions are overlapping shares, not a 100% stack. College students: types typically ≥5% of New Applications Age Total statewide — Employment and Training, Other E&T, LPIE (Local Programs that Increase Employability), employed 20 hours, care of a child, work study. Tiny or obsolete exemption columns (JTPA, Trade Act, Tribal TANF, WIOA, Title 4, state/local training, CalFresh E&T, CalGrant TANF, CalWORKs, Disabled) are omitted. SSI: Claim Homeless Deduction - SSI, Claim Shelter Deduction - SSI, Claim Standard Medical Deduction - SSI as a share of some-SSI approved + denied. Parallel SSI-only claim columns are unused here.
  • Student volume hover notes New Applications - ICT Transfers.
  • Default view is 100% (or % claiming, for exemptions). # plots the same series as counts; side-by-side student vs SSI (or two counties) are not comparable magnitudes in #, and SSI race is persons while SSI language is households.

Hover

  • Stacked mix: the count those percents are of. All applications = applications disposed. College students = applications disposed (approved or denied, not New Applications Age Total), plus still pended. SSI = household applications disposed.
  • Channels: applications received (all / SSI) or the student source total.
  • Denial details: applications denied.
  • Age / Language / Race / Gender / Household size: the count those percents (or stacked counts) are of. Students = the matching Age/Language/Race/Gender total. SSI language = households disposed. SSI race = persons in applications disposed. SSI household size = SSI-only households added. Student Age hover also has average age; SSI household-size and volume hover have SSI average age. Student volume hover notes ICT transfers.
  • Exemptions & deductions: the count those percents are of (student age total, or SSI household applications disposed). Hover lists each overlapping rate; they do not sum to 100%.
