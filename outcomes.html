Sources Data: CDSS CF296 — Applications, Expedited Service, and Recertifications, via the live CalFresh Data – Consolidated spreadsheet. All-application outcome percentages are of applications disposed that month (approved + denied ineligible + denied procedural + withdrawn). College student outcomes are from Master_Monthly (applications containing at least one student: approved, denied, or still pended). Caseload movement numbers are certified cases from Part C. Definitions County Size groups: X-Large = 6 largest counties by CalFresh households, Large > 25,000 households, Medium 5,000–25,000, Small < 5,000 — computed from the latest available month of Master_Monthly. Data notes
  • CF296 current-era (FY2025-26+) and CF296_Legacy (FY2016-17 through FY2024-25) are combined by item meaning.
  • CDSS uses “*” both for counts under 11 and for some larger complementary totals. When exactly one of the five outcome cells is starred, the missing value is recovered from the identity (disposed = approved + ineligible + procedural + withdrawn) if that recovered number is over 10. Remaining under-11 cells are plotted as 5 and called out in the hover; they are never shown as a count. Months with two or more large hidden cells, and counties CDSS listed as not reporting, stay blank.
  • Caseload movement uses CF296 Part C totals only: Item 5.a Applications approved (TOTAL / C. Total), Item 5.d Cases with eligibility reinstated and benefits pro-rated (TOTAL / C. Total), and Item 7 Cases discontinued during the month (TOTAL / C. Total). Net is additions minus discontinuances. Item 5’s other add reasons (status change, ICT, other approvals) are not included.
  • The optional applications-received chart under each outcomes trend is CF296 Item 1 (TOTAL), a count of applications filed that month. It is not the same as applications disposed that month (the stacks above it).
  • College student application columns on Master_Monthly (including unlabeled New Applications … / Denial Reason … / caseload slices in that block) are student-scoped, not the general applicant pool. The student stacked mix starts January 2023. Pended applications are still in process, so that mix is not CF296 “share of applications disposed.”
