EAs = Emergency Allotments. Data notes
  • Data reports less frequently than monthly are shown as step functions.
  • $ Issued (CalFresh benefits issued) only has county breakdowns in the CalFresh Data Dashboard from Sep 2023 onward — shown as a gap before that date for any county other than California. Historical data will be included from the DFA/CF256 report in a future build.
  • If a county's participant count jumps or drops by 40% or more for exactly one month and then returns to the surrounding trend, we treat that month as a CDSS reporting error and leave a gap in the line rather than interpolating. The newest month cannot be checked this way until the following month is published.
