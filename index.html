Sources Data: CDSS CalFresh Data Dashboard and CF296: Applications, Expedited Service, and Recertifications (ACL 22-85). Definitions CalFresh is the name for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) in California. CFAP (California Food Assistance Program) is a state-funded food benefit for people who do not qualify for federal SNAP; a person is on federal SNAP or CFAP, not both. Mixed households can have some federal members and some CFAP members. County Size groups: X-Large = 6 largest counties by CalFresh households, Large > 25,000 households, Medium 5,000–25,000, Small < 5,000 — computed from the latest available month, provisional pending final confirmation. EAs = Emergency Allotments. Data notes
  • Data reports less frequently than monthly are shown as step functions.
  • $ Issued (CalFresh benefits issued) only has county breakdowns in the CalFresh Data Dashboard from Sep 2023 onward — shown as a gap before that date for any county other than California. Historical data will be included from the DFA/CF256 report in a future build. Checking CFAP with All Participants off shows state-funded issuances (CFAP), including the CFAP slice of mixed households — not “state-only” households. SNB and TNB dollars are excluded from Total Issuances; checking SNB / TNB adds them.
  • CFAP person counts are published for California only. Checking CFAP restacks All Participants into federal SNAP and CFAP; the stacked height matches the umbrella total. Uncheck All Participants to read the CFAP band on its own scale.
  • SNB (Supplemental Nutrition Benefit) people are already inside the CalFresh persons total — a subset. TNB (Transitional Nutrition Benefit) people are not on CF 256 and are not inside that total — extra people. One SNB / TNB toggle stacks them (SNB bottom, TNB top). SNB/TNB dollars are likewise excluded from issuance totals until that toggle is on.
  • Unemployment rate is EDD county unemployment, not seasonally adjusted. It shares the right axis with $ Issued; checking one turns the other off.
  • Annual % change is versus the same month a year earlier. Quarterly % change is versus three months earlier — not a calendar-quarter average. The two % change charts keep their own Annual / Quarterly setting.
  • Caseload movement is certified cases from CF296, not the same as Total Participants (households and persons who participated that month, from the CDSS Data Dashboard Monthly tab). Each figure is in the month CDSS reported it. Other approvals and reinstated cases typically offset discontinuances from the prior month.
  • ICT in is an inter-county transfer into this county. Transfers out are already inside discontinuances. CDSS does not publish a separate ICT-out count.
Wonky & math-y details

CFAP, SNB, TNB, and $ Issued

  • Statewide, CalFresh Persons = All Federal Persons + All CFAP Persons. Checking CFAP restacks that identity; it does not add people on top of All Participants.
  • County person columns for federal/CFAP are blank. The CFAP stack is California only.
  • State Issuances are CF 256 Item 10b: state-funded issuances (CFAP), including the CFAP slice of mixed households. SNB and TNB dollars are excluded from Total Issuances until SNB / TNB is checked.
  • SNB cases are already inside regular household/person totals. TNB cases are not on CF 256 and are not inside CalFresh Persons.

Caseload movement

  • Current-era CF296 (FY2025-26+) and CF296_Legacy (FY2016-17 through FY2024-25) are combined by item meaning, not by cell number.
  • Figures are CF296 Part C, in the month CDSS reported them.
  • Adds: Item 5.a applications approved, Item 5.e Other approvals, Item 5.d reinstated prorated, Item 5.c ICT in.
  • Exits: Item 7 full discontinuances. Item 5.b is omitted (Total is 0). Item 9.b recert ineligible is not subtracted from Item 7.
  • When Part C Total is starred, it is recovered from PACF + NACF. A starred PACF or NACF part is plotted as 5.
  • Other approvals are typically rescinded discontinuances. They offset prior-month Item 7, and they stay in the report month.
  • ICT in is Item 5.c (incoming only). ICT outs are inside Item 7; CF296 has no ICT-out cell. Statewide, ICT in and those outs should cancel in the net.

One-month reporting holes

  • A month is blanked when the person count is at least 40% off the average of the months on either side, and those two neighbors are within 10% of each other. Real policy shifts fail that test because the new level lasts.
  • If at least half of counties with data that month match, every county is blanked. Feb 2019 (all counties) and Sonoma April 2019 are always included.
  • The newest month cannot be judged until the next month is published. Annual % change is computed after blanking, so both legs of the bad ratio disappear.
