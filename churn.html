What is Churn?

Sometimes CalFresh households are not able to complete their regular reporting and later come back to file a full reapplication. That cycle is churn. Households that remained eligible throughout their enrollment gap miss out on benefits. Counties must take on the extra work of processing “new” applications that could have been continued through a sometimes more streamlined recertification or SAR 7 process.

The charts below show churn in two ways:

  1. Recertification / SAR 7 Outcomes shows churning cases as a share of cases with certain reports due in a specific month.
  2. Applicants’ Prior CalFresh Connection shows churning cases as a proportion of all application processing work.

  • The two California sections share one report month and one county (California is the default). Recertification / SAR 7 Outcomes follows households scheduled in that month (Measurement 1). Combined sums Recertification and SAR 7 counts; Side by side shows the two clocks. Applicants’ Prior CalFresh Connection (Who applied) follows applications disposed in that same report month (Measurement 2). A recent recert or SAR 7 was due in the prior four months — this clock looks backward from the application, not later. Same report row, different clocks — the series are not shifted to line up. Each county lineup keeps its own filters.
  • User-facing names are SAR 7 and recertification (RRR). Source name is CF 18 CalFresh Churn Monthly Report.
  • Orange on Recertification and SAR 7 is a later new application after a missed recert or SAR 7, not a procedural denial. Eligible reapplications are churn; ineligible includes withdrawals. Grey Ineligible is the recert or SAR 7 itself. Who applied uses a forest / olive / wine / rose palette so those bands cannot be read as the due-chart colors. Average days lost (Item 7) and the dollar estimates are in the Estimated Lost Benefits fold-out. Days to approval stays on Applications.
  • CDSS hides small numbers with an asterisk. Those slices are plotted as 5 and hover percents start with ~. Item 1 (households scheduled) and Item 17 (applications disposed) are not plotted as 5 — if those totals are starred, the month is blank.
Wonky & math-y details

Measurement 1 (recertification and SAR 7 due)

  • 100% = Item 1 (C2 recertification / C1 SAR 7): households scheduled that Data Cohort Month. A household is in one column or the other, never both (ACL 18-117). Combined sums the two columns.
  • No loss of benefits = Items 2 + 4 + 6a. Late with loss = Item 6b. Item 6 = 6a + 6b exactly; the chart uses the split. Item 7 (average days of lost benefits) is in Estimated Lost Benefits.
  • Recertification / SAR 7 ineligible = Items 3 + 5 + 8.
  • Reapplications = Items 9–16. Reapplication Outcomes is eligible vs ineligible of those. Time to reapply is the next / 2 / 3 / 4 calendar months after the due month (% of Items 9–16). Lag months are calendar months, not 1–30 / 31–90 days.
  • No renewal and no return within 4 months = Item 1 minus the bands above. Item 1 is not an auto-sum of the outcome cells. If the parts overshoot Item 1, that month is left blank.
  • Lost-benefits dollars: Item 6b households × (Item 7 days ÷ 30) × average issuance per household, plus Items 9+11+13+15 × 1/2/3/4 months of lag × the same rate. Average issuance is California Total Issuances ÷ CalFresh Households; if that month is missing, the latest month at or before it. Does not include no-return or ineligible reapplications.

Measurement 2 (who applied)

  • 100% = Item 17 (C37): CalFresh/CFAP applications disposed that month.
  • New / not recently on = Item 17 − Item 18. Other Recently On = Item 18 − Item 19. Item 18 is participated in the prior four months.
  • Recent SAR 7 Due = C39. Recent Recert Due = C40. Restoration and 1 vs 2–4 months prior (approved vs denied) are ACL Items 20–28 — the case was restored or closed before the application, not after.
  • Sort by Recent Recert & SAR 7 Due is C39 + C40 (combined counts). Counties with a starred Item 17 are blank.

Small cells (*)

  • Starred part cells under 11 are plotted as 5. Hover percents then start with ~.
  • Do not impute 5 on a starred Item 1 or Item 17 total — those stars are often complementary large cells. The month stays blank.
